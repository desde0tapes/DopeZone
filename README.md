# DopeZone

Position based Tricking (Gap style) add-on to BRC. Earns HyperBoost units for HyperBoostMod.

### What Is it?

DopeZone its a trick counter tied to the position where the "gap" or "zone" starts meant to earn HyperBoost Units. 

If you leave the zone your Dope combo breaks!

Grinding or sliding doesnt mean your Dope combo will be active! You have to be constantly tricking!

Perform the required tricks in the zone to get your HyperBoost Units!

Value of tricks:

Regular Tricks = 1 trick
Boost Tricks = 2 tricks
High jumps or Dope Jumps = 4 tricks

Streak:

Obtaining 5 rewards of HB units in a row will give you a total x2 Multiplier !

Multipliers:

Doing 1/3 of your required tricks with Dope Drops will give you a x2 Dope Master multiplier on each HB reward!

### What are High Jumps and Dope Jumps?

These have x4 the value a regular trick so they'll get you faster to the HB reward.

High jumps are the vanilla "trick jump" that happens when you press jump right after a ground trick.

Dope Jumps on the other side, are those tricks performed withing the same frame after a jump. They happen when you press a trick right after a jump, almost at the same time but still being the jump the first to be pressed — Almost in the same frame period.

### What are Dope Drops?

Performing a bunch of these in your Dope combo will give you a x2 reward, they gain you more HB units per reward but require more precision.

To obtain, go down any gap, big step, ledge, grind, etc. WITHOUT jumping but still tricking. Trick must be done right in the time period of leaving the ground (like in real life) + trick must be completed entirely / not interrupted by the ground.
